1. Introduction

Welcome to Udodiri Young Social Club ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. 2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Account Registration: Name, email address, phone number, profile picture, and membership information
  • Authentication Data: Login credentials and two-factor authentication information
  • Communication: Messages, announcements, and chat data within the brotherhood chat and broadcast systems
  • Financial Information: Payment methods, subscription details, dues, levies, and financial records (processed securely through Paystack and Flutterwave)
  • Event Information: Calendar entries, meeting minutes, and event participation data
  • Uploaded Content: Documents, images, and other content you upload to the platform

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Service, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, clicks, interactions, and features used
  • Location Data: IP address and approximate geographic location (if permitted)
  • Log Data: Server logs, access times, referrer pages, and error information

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Google OAuth: When you use Google authentication, Google shares your email, name, and profile information
  • Payment Processors: Paystack and Flutterwave may share transaction status and verification data
  • Other Users: Information you may be mentioned or tagged in by other members

4. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and destruction. These include:

  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • AWS Security: Leveraging AWS's enterprise-grade security infrastructure (DynamoDB encryption, Secrets Manager, IAM policies)
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control and principle of least privilege
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Secure Authentication: AWS Cognito for secure user authentication and session management

Important: While we implement robust security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and report any suspicious activity immediately.
